The two problems that dominate everything

P0 · Brand discoverability

The name resolves to a medical term

Search and AI engines read "agensis" as a misspelling of agenesis (an organ failing to develop). "agensis" returns zero brand results and a page of pathology; LLMs auto-correct the name before they ever consider citing you. This is a compounding tax on recall, search, and AI citation. It does not force a rename, but the entity + disambiguation work is non-negotiable and urgent.

P0 · Retrievability

There is nothing for humans or models to retrieve

One indexable page, no entity node (no Wikidata, no Organization schema), and no third-party footprint (no Product Hunt, Crunchbase, G2, GitHub, press, reviews). AI answer engines have nothing to cite for exactly the AI-native buyer agensis targets. Until the corpus exists, agensis is invisible where it most needs to be seen.

What's already working

Keep these: the homepage is genuinely server-rendered with real content (crawlable), has a clean single-H1 structure, a canonical tag, and OG title/description. The copy voice is distinctive and an asset ("Everything a colleague needs. Nothing a chatbot has." / "Put agents on the roster."). The foundation is better than most pre-launch products.

The defensible wedge

The category is crowded and shadowed by giants. OpenAgents (open-source "Collaboration OS for AI Agents") is the closest rival; Slack Agentforce and Microsoft ship "agent-first workspace" as a feature of tools teams already pay for. The threat is the category collapsing into an incumbent checkbox.

What incumbents structurally can't say: open, neutral, MCP-native, model-agnostic. Pair that with the agent-to-agent mesh and live presence, and the story becomes "the open, vendor-neutral place where any agent from any model works together." That is the wedge the current site buries under vibe.

Canonical definition (use verbatim everywhere)

agensis is an agentic workspace where humans and AI agents work together in channels and threads, with persistent memory, live presence, and a shared canvas, built on an open, MCP-native, model-agnostic runtime.

Byte-identical across site, schema, Wikidata, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, and G2. Consistency is how LLMs merge you into one strong entity instead of several weak ones.

90-day path to spec

Phase 1 · weeks 1–2 · foundation & disambiguation
  • Kill the soft-404; move the app to app.agensis.io; ship real robots.txt + sitemap.xml.
  • Add the JSON-LD schema graph with sameAs[] on every page.
  • Create the Wikidata item; claim LinkedIn / Crunchbase / GitHub / Product Hunt / G2 with the identical name lockup + definition.
  • Add waitlist + design-partner capture; add the canonical definition; unify the two site descriptions.
  • Decide the beachhead ICP; write the one-page positioning doc.
Phase 2 · weeks 3–6 · depth, conversion, proof
  • Build real content pages + FAQ blocks + comparison/alternatives pages (vs OpenAgents, vs Agentforce).
  • Add og:image + Twitter cards; baseline Core Web Vitals (watch INP on the SPA).
  • Rebuild the homepage to the conversion spec: clarity subhead, social proof, use cases, demo, one repeated primary CTA.
  • Write the messaging house; sharpen the open-runtime wedge in all copy; ship a 60s demo + first design-partner logos.
Phase 3 · weeks 7–12 · corpus & launch
  • Product Hunt + Show HN launch; seed G2/Capterra reviews; earn listicle inclusion + mentions; participate on Reddit/HN/Discord.
  • Publish original data on multi-agent coordination; ship llms.txt + llms-full.txt.
  • Stand up AI-visibility tracking + AI-crawler log monitoring + GA4 AI referral segment; build battlecards; add a beta pricing story.

Founder decisions that unblock the set

1 · Beachhead ICP

Dev teams, agencies running client agents, or technical founders. Pick one.

2 · Naming posture

Keep the name but always ship it as "agensis — agentic workspace" and win Wikidata fast.

3 · Open-core or closed

The biggest lever against open-source OpenAgents.

4 · Beta / free story

Replace "no pricing games yet" with an explicit free-during-beta promise + design partners.
